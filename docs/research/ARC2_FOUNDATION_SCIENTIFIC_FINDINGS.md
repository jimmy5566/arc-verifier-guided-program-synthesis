# ARC2 Foundation Scientific Findings

This document consolidates public, versioned development evidence. It is not a hidden-test claim or an ARC-AGI-2 generalization result.

- Family-Balanced improved teacher-forced likelihood on reused development evidence without a corresponding Rank-1 exact-grid gain. This is a likelihood-to-decision gap observation, not evidence that a preference objective is causal.
- E04-E equal-slot marker replay improved marker binding within its matched E04 development protocol. Its transfer beyond that distribution is not established.
- The historical E05 construction is not construct-identifiable for rotation transfer: the plus pentomino is quarter-turn invariant and target color roles are confounded. It must not be used as clean evidence for or against structural transfer.
- E4-G Inside/Contains reached 46/48 train fit and showed an apparent +20/48 native-OOD gain, but failed its frozen joint decision because retention was 0, composition was -1, and order invariance was only 0.652. The original `REJECT` is preserved.
- A post-result audit established that all 48 E4-G primary training tasks used demo order A, demo 1 matched the query operation in 48/48 tasks, both train and OOD used only color pairs (1,2) and (4,6), and frame color was numerically smaller than inner color in all 96 OOD records. Marker plus numeric color ordering reconstructed all 96 old OOD targets without spatial-relation reasoning.
- E4-H evaluated frozen V7 and E4-G weights without retraining on 48 fresh paired canonical units with counterbalanced color order, unseen color pairs, fixed A/B demonstrations, equal color counts, and controlled structure. E4-G improved the strict both-order endpoint by only +5/48, paired 95% bootstrap CI [0,11]. Its gain was +12 records when frame color was numerically smaller but only +3 under reversed ordering, and order invariance remained 0.462. This is shortcut-sensitive evidence, not confirmed independent transfer.
- Historical Foundation-V2 diagnostics indicate weak connected-component/object-extraction and relation primitives. These observations motivate, but do not yet authorize, matched training.

No checkpoint promotion follows from these findings. E4-H raw predictions were frozen before its deterministic target sidecar was derived; Gold, dGold, FINAL_AUDIT, and official submission paths remained unopened.
