# ARC2 Foundation Research Roadmap

1. Complete the frozen V4 three-checkpoint OOD measurement only after fresh, SHA-bound Director authorization. Its primary outcome is native-Greedy exact-grid behavior with fixed Batch1 sensitivity evidence and protected target isolation.
2. If V4 fails to establish transferable Treatment benefit, freeze a small matched Connected Components/Object Extraction training study from V7. The control and treatment must share optimizer, updates, supervised-token budget, and protected replay; structural diversity is the only proposed variable.
3. Evaluate any selected checkpoint with independently frozen OOD transfer and protected retention. Report protected families separately.
4. Choose a second intervention only after the updated result review. Relation/selection and conditional-action probes remain deferred.

No training, checkpoint promotion, or new cohort is authorized by this roadmap.
