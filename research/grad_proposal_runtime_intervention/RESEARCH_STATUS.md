# Research status: frozen preliminary evidence

## Working direction

**Responsibility-Aware Runtime Intervention for Interactive LLM Agents**

## Current core finding

Component-level responsibility attribution provides decision-relevant
information beyond generic uncertainty when choosing heterogeneous, costly
runtime interventions.

## Frozen evidence status

- **Stage-0:** responsibility mismatch demonstrated in frozen ARC candidate
  cohorts; it motivates, but does not validate, interactive-agent attribution.
- **Stage-1:** telemetry-based attribution is feasible in a synthetic,
  single-source CPU-only interactive sandbox.
- **Stage-2A:** better calibration alone did not guarantee better decisions.
- **Stage-2B:** verification cost and repair effectiveness determine whether
  attribution-aware verification has positive utility.
- **Stage-2C:** explicit responsibility attribution improved heterogeneous
  multi-intervention routing beyond uncertainty-only and simple baselines.
- **Stage-3:** the result remained positive under dual faults, limited budgets,
  overlapping intervention effects, imperfect marginal attribution, and an OOD
  failure-prevalence shift.

## Final current research gate

**GO for proposal/preliminary evidence.** No further benchmark or LLM
experiment is required at the application stage.

## Frozen boundary

This archive does not change, reinterpret as production evidence, rerun, or
depend on any Kaggle competition/submission pipeline. The present claim is
limited to controlled preliminary/synthetic evidence and proposal motivation.
