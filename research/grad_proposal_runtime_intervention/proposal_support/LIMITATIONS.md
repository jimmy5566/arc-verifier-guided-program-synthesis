# Limitations and claim boundary

1. ARC evidence is motivation for runtime responsibility attribution; it is not
   interactive-agent validation.
2. Stage-1 through Stage-3 are synthetic CPU-only environments.
3. Attribution labels are simulator-grounded rather than collected from real
   LLM-agent executions.
4. Real LLM-agent ecological validity is not established.
5. The Stage-3 environment has at most dual faults, independent partial-repair
   assumptions, and a fixed intervention effect/cost matrix.
6. These experiments support a proposal and preliminary methodology. They do
   not constitute a final thesis result, a deployed system, or a general claim
   about all interactive LLM agents.
